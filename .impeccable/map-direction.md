# Map experience direction

Mode: Operate. Incumbent styles and supplied mascot are visual authority; this expands their application to a map interface.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Discover the circuit spatially, then make a deliberate stop; geography occupies the main workspace.
OWN-WORLD: Existing deep teal, warm green-white surfaces, terracotta for attractions, readable sans controls and incumbent serif headings. Supplied mascot remains the guide.
STORY: See the territory, select a verified place, understand a road route, and confirm interesting stops.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact full-width branded header; broad map on the left with search and sector controls; one right-side panel for list, destination, itinerary and Quíbar. On narrow screens the panel follows the map.
FORM: Map workspace and persistent task panel; no new branding, blocking welcome, fabricated map artwork or automatic route changes.
FINISH: Review the finished desktop and mobile compositions, document shipped behavior in DESIGN.md, and retain provenance for supplied assets. No new raster art is needed.
